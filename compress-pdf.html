Frequently asked questions

Is the PDF compressor free?

Yes. There is no sign-up, no watermark and no limit on how many times you can use it.

Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

No. Leafpress compresses your PDF inside your browser, on your own device. The file is never sent to us or anyone else, so it is suitable for private documents.

How does the compression work?

Each page is redrawn as an image at a lower resolution, saved with JPEG compression, and the pages are joined into a new PDF. This works very well for scans and PDFs full of photos.

Can I still select and search the text afterwards?

No. Because the pages become images, text can no longer be selected, copied or searched, and links and form fields stop working. Keep your original file if you need those.

Why did my PDF not get smaller?

PDFs made mostly of text and simple graphics are already small, and turning them into images would make them bigger. When that happens Leafpress tells you and does not offer the larger file.

Which compression level should I choose?

Recommended suits most email and online forms. Strong gives the smallest file for on-screen reading. Light keeps pages sharper if you may print them.

Can I compress a password-protected PDF?

Not directly. Remove the password first, then compress the unlocked copy.

Is there a size limit?

Leafpress does not set a limit. The practical limit is your device's memory, and large PDFs with hundreds of pages take longer, so split very big files into parts if it stalls.
