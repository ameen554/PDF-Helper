Frequently asked questions

Is the PDF to Excel converter free?

Yes. There is no sign-up, no watermark and no limit on how many PDFs you can convert.

Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

No. Leafpress reads your PDF and builds the Excel file inside your browser, on your own device. The file is never sent to us or anyone else, so it is suitable for private documents.

Which PDFs work best?

PDFs made on a computer, such as invoices, price lists, bank statements and reports exported from Word, Excel or accounting software. They contain real text, which Leafpress can read together with its position on the page.

Can it convert scanned PDFs?

Not yet. A scanned PDF is a picture of a page, and turning a picture into cells needs OCR (text recognition), which Leafpress does not do. A quick check: if you cannot select text in the PDF with your mouse, it is a scan.

How does it find the columns?

Leafpress looks at where the text sits on the page. Text on the same line becomes a row, and text that lines up down the page becomes a column. If columns are joined or split in the wrong places, change Column detection to Split more or Split less and convert again.

Will colours, borders and merged cells be kept?

No. You get the text and numbers in cells, without the formatting of the original. That keeps the sheet clean and easy to edit.

Why are some numbers left as text?

With the number option on, values such as 1,234.50, (45.00) and 12% become Excel numbers, and currency symbols and thousands separators are removed so you can calculate with them. Codes with leading zeros, very long numbers such as account numbers, dates, and anything mixed with letters stay as text so they are not changed. Turn the option off to keep everything exactly as written.

Why does one table cell appear on several rows?

When text wraps over several lines inside a cell in the PDF, each line is read as its own row. You can join them in Excel after converting.

Is there a page limit?

You can convert up to 300 pages at a time. For a longer PDF, use the Pages box to convert it in parts.
