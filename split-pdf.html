    Frequently asked questions

    Is the PDF splitter free?

    Yes. There is no sign-up, no watermark and no limit on how many times you can use it.

    Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

    No. Leafpress splits your PDF inside your browser, on your own device. The file is never sent to us or anyone else, so it is suitable for private documents.

    What is the difference between the three ways to split?

    Extract pages keeps only the pages you list and puts them in one new PDF. Split by ranges makes a separate PDF for every range you type, such as 1-3, 4-6. Every N pages cuts the whole document into equal parts, and choosing 1 gives you one PDF per page.

    How do I type the pages?

    Use numbers, ranges and commas, for example 1-3, 5, 8-. A range ending in a dash, such as 8-, runs to the last page. In Extract mode the pages are added in the order you type them.

    How can I delete pages from a PDF?

    Use Extract pages and list the pages you want to keep. To remove page 3 from a 10-page PDF, type 1-2, 4-10.

    Will the text stay sharp and selectable?

    Yes. Pages are copied into the new files exactly as they are, not turned into pictures, so text stays sharp and searchable.

    Can I split a password-protected PDF?

    Not directly. Remove the password first, then split the unlocked copy.

    What is not kept when I split?

    Every page, with its text, images and drawings, is kept. Extras that belong to the whole document, such as bookmarks and fillable form fields, and links that jump between pages, may not carry over.

    Is there a limit?

    Leafpress does not limit the size of your PDF, but one run can create up to 300 files. The practical limit is your device's memory, so if a very large PDF stalls, split it in stages.
