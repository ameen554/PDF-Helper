Privacy policy

Short version: the files you convert stay on your device.

Last updated: September 20, 2026

Files you convert

Leafpress tools run entirely in your web browser. When you add images, they are read on your own device and turned into a PDF there. The files are not uploaded to our servers or shared with anyone. When you close or reload the page, they are gone from the tool.

Information we collect

Leafpress does not ask you to create an account and does not collect personal information such as your name or email address through the tools.

Like most websites, the server that hosts Leafpress may keep standard technical logs, such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested. These are used to keep the site secure and running, and are handled under our hosting provider's policies.

Cookies and tracking

Leafpress does not use cookies for tracking or advertising. If this changes, for example by adding analytics or ads, this page will be updated and, where required, you will be asked for consent.

Third-party services

The pages load fonts and scripts from Leafpress itself and do not call outside services to process your files.

Children

Leafpress is a general-purpose tool and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Leafpress handles data, we will update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email hello@yourdomain.com.
