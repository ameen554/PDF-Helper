    Frequently asked questions

    Is the PDF to image converter free?

    Yes. There is no sign-up, no watermark and no limit on how many PDFs you can convert.

    Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

    No. Leafpress converts your PDF inside your browser, on your own device. The file is never sent to us or anyone else, so it is suitable for private documents.

    Which image formats can I save as?

    PNG is lossless and best for text and sharp graphics. JPG gives smaller files and suits pages with photos. WebP is smallest, and is available in browsers that can create it.

    What resolution should I choose?

    150 dpi is a good all-round choice for screens and sharing. Use 72 dpi for small previews, and 200 or 300 dpi when you need to zoom in or print.

    Can I convert only some pages?

    Yes. Type the pages you want in the Pages box, for example 1-3, 5, and leave it empty to convert every page.

    Will the text in the images be selectable?

    No. Each page becomes a picture, so text can no longer be selected or searched. Keep your original PDF if you need that.

    Can I convert a password-protected PDF?

    Not directly. Remove the password first, then convert the unlocked copy.

    Why is a very large page smaller than expected?

    To work in every browser, pages that would be larger than about 16 megapixels, such as posters at 300 dpi, are reduced to that size.

    Is there a page limit?

    You can convert up to 200 pages at a time. For a longer PDF, use the Pages box to do it in parts.
