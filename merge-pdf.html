    How to merge PDF files

    1. Add your PDFs

      Drop two or more PDFs onto the box, or choose them from your device. Add more at any time.

    2. Put them in order

      Drag files up or down, or use the arrows. Each row shows its page count, and the numbers show the final order.

    3. Merge and download

      Select Merge PDFs. The combined file is built on your device and is ready to save in a moment.

    One tidy PDF, without the upload

    Frequently asked questions

    Is the PDF merger free?

    Yes. There is no sign-up, no watermark and no limit on how many times you can use it.

    Are my PDFs uploaded to a server?

    No. Leafpress merges your PDFs inside your browser, on your own device. The files are never sent to us or anyone else, so it is suitable for private documents.

    Will the text stay sharp and selectable?

    Yes. Pages are copied into the new file exactly as they are, not turned into pictures. Text stays sharp and searchable, and the merged file is roughly as large as the originals added together.

    How do I change the order of the files?

    Drag the files up or down in the list, or use the arrow buttons on each row. The number on each file shows its place in the merged PDF.

    Can I merge password-protected PDFs?

    Not directly. Remove the password first, then add the unlocked copy.

    What is not kept when I merge?

    Every page, with its text, images and drawings, is kept. Extras that belong to the whole document, such as bookmarks and fillable form fields, and links that jump between pages, may not carry over.

    Can I merge images with my PDFs?

    Not in this tool. Use the Image to PDF tool to turn your pictures into a PDF first, then add that file here.

    Is there a limit on the number or size of files?

    Leafpress does not set a limit. The practical limit is your device's memory, so if a very large batch stalls, merge it in smaller groups and then merge the results.
