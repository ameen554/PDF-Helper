    Frequently asked questions

    Is the image compressor free?

    Yes. There is no sign-up, no watermark and no limit on how many images you can compress.

    Are my images uploaded to a server?

    No. Leafpress compresses your images inside your browser, on your own device. The files are never sent to us or anyone else.

    Which image formats can I compress?

    JPG, PNG, WebP, AVIF and BMP work in most modern browsers. HEIC photos from an iPhone open only in Safari. Animated GIFs are not supported because they would lose their animation. You can save the results as JPG, WebP or PNG.

    What quality setting should I use?

    For photos, 70 to 80 usually looks the same as the original at a much smaller size. Go lower for the smallest files, and higher for images with fine detail such as text or line art.

    Will I lose image quality?

    JPG and WebP are lossy formats, so a little detail is discarded to save space. At the default setting the difference is hard to see. PNG is lossless, so PNG files keep their exact pixels and only get smaller if you also reduce their size. Choose WebP for much smaller files.

    What happens to transparent images?

    PNG and WebP keep transparency. JPG cannot be see-through, so transparent areas turn white if you save as JPG.

    Why was my image not made smaller?

    Some images are already well optimised. When the new file would not be smaller, Leafpress keeps your original so you never get a bigger file.

    Are photo details like location removed?

    Yes. Each image is redrawn, which removes hidden information such as camera details and GPS location. The picture is still shown the right way up.

    Is there a limit on image size?

    Leafpress does not limit the number of files. To work in every browser, photos larger than about 16 megapixels are reduced to that size. If a large batch stalls, compress it in smaller groups.
